### DOLA | mint inflation, fee manipulation | WATCH

**Three things moved that were all quiet last week, and none of them appears in any proposal.**

**DELIVERED: 5,000,000 DOLA minted, in one transaction, by the Fed chair multisig.** At block
26,075,996 a single batch made two `expansion` calls, each gated `require(msg.sender == chair)`:
2,000,000 into the yvCurve-DOLA-sUSDS market and 3,000,000 into the DOLA-sUSDS market.

- **The ceilings did not move.** Both markets sat at 50,000,000 at each end, read as state because
  the ceiling setter emits no event. The mints were 4.0% and 6.0% of those ceilings, taking the two
  markets to 8.2% and 21.6% of them.

  5,000,000 went straight to idle**, taking idle DOLA in the two markets from 3,825,569.96 to

**DELIVERED: the DBR emission rate rose 51,000,000 to 55,000,000** at block 26,045,717, by the
chair acting as the auction operator. The cap of 200,000,000 did not move, so the raise uses 27.5%
of it. **Direction matters and is benign: a higher rate cheapens DBR and therefore cheapens FiRM
borrowing. The harmful direction is a cut toward zero.** The four-week sequence is now 45M, 47M,
51M, flat, 55M.

**DELIVERED: a second DBR auction was quadrupled**, 500,000 to 2,000,000, at block 26,054,356.
Its operator is the multisig that also manages the Monolith sister protocol, but **the auction is
Inverse-side**: its asset is INV, its DBR is the FiRM DBR, and its governor is the Inverse
timelock. It is counted once here and not against Monolith. Its own cap of 50,000,000 is unchanged.
Separately, a permissionless sweep moved 56,808.15 DOLA from the main auction to its sale handler;
that call carries no access control and the same multisig happened to be the caller.

**WHAT IT IS WORTH, and the honest framing in both directions.** Governance authorised neither
call: the proposal count is 375 at both ends, there were zero governance logs, and **no recent
proposal's action list contains an expansion call or any call to the DBR auction at all.** **But
none of it is a circumvention.** Minting into a market is the only power the chair role has,
granted long ago; setting the rate is the auction operator's standing mandate. And the division is
demonstrable rather than asserted: **the only Fed calls governance has made lately set the market
ceilings**, which four of the ten most recent proposals do, and those ceilings did not move.

**WHAT IT DOES NOT COVER.** No role, admin, pointer or threshold changed anywhere on this surface:
the governor, chair, operator and minter flags all read identically at both ends.

**WHAT TO WATCH.** Whether the idle 8,674,435.46 is drawn or recalled; whether the rate moves a
fourth time and in which direction, since a cut is the harmful one.

### USDe | mint inflation | WATCH

**No authority over minting changed. What changed is throughput.**

**DELIVERED: the per-block redeem cap was doubled, 10,000,000 to 20,000,000 USDe**, scheduled at
block 26,031,963 and executed at 26,040,398, **28.3 hours later through the full 24-hour lane**.
The per-asset cap doubled on six of the seven collateral assets; DAI was held at 2,000,000.

- **What it is worth to a lender:** the cap is the ceiling on how fast backing can leave, and so
  the floor on how long a full drain would take. Doubling it halves that floor, from roughly 98
  minutes to roughly 49. It bounds Ethena's own whitelisted redeemers rather than an outsider,
  since redemption is role-gated, and it is a throughput control rather than an authority. It went
  through the delayed lane, where it could be seen coming.
- **What it does not cover:** the mint-side caps did not move, and the redeem cap has no ceiling
  of its own in code.

**ADDED through the ZERO-delay lane, in the same week:** two brand-new plain keys gained benefactor
rights, and one of them has since bridged in 49,990 USDe and granted the minting contract an
unlimited allowance without yet minting or redeeming. A benefactor holds no role and cannot mint;
it is the counterparty that may be named in a mint or redeem order. **Also through that lane, 18
cross-chain peer routes were cut to zero** in a single transaction, 18 blocks after the first
benefactor grant, covering six destinations across three token adapters. A further endpoint was
separately throttled to zero throughput through the delayed lane. Severing routes narrows the
surface rather than widening it.

**The authority answer:** the owner is the timelock, the pending owner is zero, the minter pointer
is unchanged, and there were zero role grants or revocations on the token, the minting contract or
the timelock. Supply grew 21,144,450.00 to 4,904,696,584.80 (+0.43%), all through the one
authorised minter.
