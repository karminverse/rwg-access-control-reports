## Changed (4)

### re.xyz reUSD | collateral valuation | WATCH

re.xyz reUSD is in assessment with no live FiRM market, so none of this is live lender exposure.
It is tracked because the price feed bears on whether the asset is suitable.

**1. The price-write configuration was changed by the multisig, and we could not see it.**

Both oracle report providers received a configuration change executed by the Re 3-of-5
Safe: a flag-like event set at block 25,676,400 alongside an event carrying two
workflow-shaped identifiers, then the same flag cleared at block 25,676,675, about 55
minutes later. Both transactions were multisig executions relayed by two different owners.
The shape is a pause, reconfigure, unpause sequence against the contracts that decide which
off-chain workflow may write re.xyz reUSD's NAV.

- **It was instant and outside governance.** The 2-day timelock was not involved and could
  not have been: it holds no role on these contracts.
- **We cannot name the exact function.** Both providers are unverified, with no published
  source or ABI, so the scanner produced no deltas for them at all and the change is only
  visible as raw logs. This report states what the logs prove and does not guess beyond it.
- **The resulting feed is clean, verified tick by tick.** Seven daily updates at gapless
  ~23.9 hour spacing with no missed day at the window seam, strictly increasing, per-tick
  moves of 1.70 to 1.71 basis points against a 0.50% band, and a weekly total of
  **+0.11937%**, on trend with the pre-migration rate. All seven were delivered through the
  Chainlink forwarder, carried the expected adapter as writer, bound to the same workflow
  identity and feed id, and each carried a full 4-of-10 signature quorum. The quorum config
  itself predates this window and did not change.
- **Net:** the outcome was benign and the price series is trustworthy for the period. The
  finding is that authority over that series can be reconfigured instantly by a 3-of-5
  multisig, and that our tooling is blind to it.

**2. A pending operation would remove that multisig's admin power over both tokens.**

A governance operation is scheduled, not executed, on re.xyz reUSD's 2-day timelock: two calls
revoking DEFAULT_ADMIN from the 3-of-5 Safe, one against the senior re.xyz reUSD token and one
against the junior reUSDe token. It becomes executable **2026-08-12 14:01:35 UTC**.

- **It is substantive, not cosmetic.** This was proven by simulation rather than inference:
  scheduling at zero delay reverts, the Safe cannot lower the minimum delay, and it cannot
  grant itself timelock admin. There is no bypass lane. What becomes delay-gated is the
  instant power to grant MINTER and UPGRADER on both tokens, which is precisely the power
  used last period to add a cross-chain minter without any waiting period.
- **Three qualifications.** Admin is narrowed, not destroyed: the 2-day timelock keeps
  DEFAULT_ADMIN on both tokens. The Safe scheduled it itself and holds the canceller role,
  so it can withdraw it before the deadline; treat it as pending, not banked. And it does
  **not** touch the price-setting contract, where the Safe remains sole admin and keeps an
  uncapped write, a surface the timelock cannot reach at all.
- Nothing has executed yet: zero role events on either token this period, implementations
  byte-identical at both endpoints.
